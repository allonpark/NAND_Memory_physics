GIDL

BTBT hole generation → channel boosting → tunnel-oxide erase field → aging/architecture trade-off를 추적합니다.

ContentsConsensusBoundaryNext DOE

Current consensus

GIDL erase는 fixed external voltage보다 local junction field, generated-hole current, channel capacitance, leakage path의 동적 balance로 보는 것이 적절합니다. cycling과 stack-height가 같은 GV에서 Vch ramp를 바꿀 수 있습니다.

Critical boundary

Adaptive GV가 Vch를 회복해도 erase ΔVTH가 회복되지 않으면 bottleneck이 GIDL generation에서 CTL/tunnel-oxide kinetics로 이동한 것입니다. 이 경우 추가 GV는 damage만 키울 수 있습니다.

Recommended discriminating DOE

Fixed vs adaptive GV, fresh/aged, stack-height, temperature split에서 I_GIDL(V), Vch(t), erase loops, select-gate leakage, SILC, retention tail을 동시에 측정합니다.

W43 Evidence Update · 19 Oct 2026

Consensus / Open Questions / Contradictions / Validated Models / Domain Boundaries / DOE

W43의 MH–WL hotspot은 GIDL erase에서의 정상 hole generation을 직접 측정한 것이 아니다. Select-gate HCI와 channel boosting을 별도로 계측한다.

English. An MH–WL hotspot is not a direct measurement of normal GIDL-erase hole generation. Select-gate HCI and channel boosting must be characterized separately.

Contradiction / Critical Boundary: Contradiction: leakage hotspot versus transient GIDL current; source does not establish carrier polarity or injection site.

Recommended DOE: DOE: SSL/GSL ramp × WL state × layer × temperature, monitor Vch(t), Ig, erase VTH and post-stress SG leakage.
