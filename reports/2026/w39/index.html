Executive Summary

W39에서 가장 중요한 변화는 stress amplitude만 맞추면 lifetime physics도 맞는다는 가정이 더 약해졌다는 것입니다. 9월 14일 발표된 TDDB 연구는 전통적인 inverse-area scaling을 defect-generation only의 필연적 결과가 아니라 generation–annihilation competition의 특수한 한계로 재해석합니다. 9월 10일 GaN 연구는 sequential stress에서 이전 bias가 만든 trap state가 이후 stress response를 바꾸므로, voltage acceleration curve 자체가 stress order에 의존할 수 있음을 실험적으로 보여줍니다.

1. TDDB area scaling is not necessarily a one-way weakest-link law

Source / what is new
Ernest Y. Wu & Takashi Ando, Statistics and physics of area-dependent dielectric breakdown: an overlooked yet inevitable natural phenomenon, Frontiers in Nanotechnology 8 (2026), published 14 Sep 2026, DOI 10.3389/fnano.2026.1885901. Hydrogen-doped HfO2 data are modeled by competing defect generation and annihilation; reverse/diminished area scaling can emerge, and classical inverse-area scaling is recovered as a limiting case.
Fig. 1 — mechanism redraw. Source numeric curves are not reconstructed. Red defects and green annihilation centers are conceptual. The figure explains why increasing area increases both candidate filament events and annihilation opportunities.
F(t)=1−exp[−(t/η)β] ; classical weakest-link area conversion assumes independent identical sub-areas

Causal chain. Field/injection creates precursor defects → many near-breakdown paths compete → some defects are annihilated/recombined before a percolation path survives → annihilation-center population itself decays with time → the effective survivor statistics become a joint minimum/maximum-value problem → apparent area scaling can weaken or reverse.

Dominant parameters. stressed area A, β, defect-generation rate, annihilation time distribution, H/O-related defect chemistry, first-SBD versus HBD criterion. The source connects estimated annihilation times with hydrogen substitution into oxygen vacancies over roughly ns-to-s timescales; this is material-specific evidence, not a universal oxide constant.

Domain boundary gate
Source evidence is H-doped HfO2/high-k breakdown. Applying the model directly to VNAND SiO2/SiN/Al2O3 or BEOL ILD is EXTRAPOLATION ONLY until reverse/diminished area scaling or recovery/annihilation signatures are independently observed.
DOE / falsification
For the user's M2M or oxide TDDB context, use ≥3 stressed areas at matched E,T and identical current criterion. Compare β and η after conventional area normalization. If all CDFs collapse with η∝A−1/β and no transient recovery/SBD disappearance exists, the annihilation extension is unnecessary in that domain. If β/curvature or normalized η changes systematically with area, investigate competing populations before user extrapolation.

2. GaN reliability has memory: prior electrical state changes later stress response

Source / what is new
Trap Dynamics and Long-Term Reliability in Depletion-Mode GaN HEMTs Under Sequential Electrical Stress, Energies 19(18), 4286, published 10 Sep 2026, DOI 10.3390/en19184286. Three nominally identical GaN-on-SiC HEMTs underwent the same sequential gate-bias/current protocol. −5 to −7 V segments produced stronger evolution than later −8/−9 V segments; +2 V produced partial VTH/RDS recovery. Authors explicitly warn not to interpret this as intrinsic non-monotonic voltage acceleration because stress order matters.
Fig. 2 — stress-history mechanism map. Qualitative timeline; no source curve reconstructed. The hidden state x(t) represents trap occupancy/reversible charge/persistent defects established by previous stress.
dx/dt = C(V,T,x)[1−x] − E(V,T,x)x + Gdefect(V,T)

이 식의 핵심은 capture/emission rate가 현재 V,T뿐 아니라 이미 채워진 trap state x에도 의존한다는 것입니다. 따라서 동일한 −8 V라도 virgin device와 −5→−7 V를 이미 거친 device의 ΔVTH(t)는 같을 필요가 없습니다.

Critical boundary
이 논문은 sequential protocol이며 independent fresh populations를 voltage별로 stress한 acceleration experiment가 아닙니다. 따라서 −8/−9 V의 작은 incremental drift를 'higher voltage causes less damage'로 해석하면 오류입니다.
DOE
Fresh-population parallel stress와 randomized/reversed sequence를 병행하고, μs→hours recovery에서 VTH/RDS/IGS를 동시에 추적합니다. 순서를 바꾸면 response가 바뀌되 fresh-population acceleration은 monotonic이면 history-state model이 지지됩니다.

3. Local strain mitigation becomes an interface-reliability knob in GaN

Source / reported
Xin Luo et al., Enhanced threshold-voltage stability via local strain mitigation in GaN HEMTs with a crystalline SiN/AlN cap, Applied Physics Letters 129, 102103 (2026), published 9 Sep 2026, DOI 10.1063/5.0334214. Geometric-phase analysis indicates that an AlN sub-cap makes strain at the crystalline-SiN/AlGaN interface more uniform. The AlN split reports lower RON, higher mobility, ΔVTH≈−0.05 V under thermal stimulation at 25/150°C and ΔVTH≈0.02 V after 1000 s at VGS=+2 V.
Fig. 3 — structure/causal redraw. Layer sequence and reported stability are source-grounded; geometry is schematic and not to scale.
ΔVTH ≈ −ΔQit/Cg,eff + ΔVpolarization/strain

Competing mechanisms. Improved VTH stability can arise from lower interface-state density, modified polarization/2DEG electrostatics, or altered injection barrier. Mobility/RON improvement supports an interface-quality contribution, but this does not by itself prove improved TDDB.

Domain boundary
+2 V / 1000 s gate-bias stability and thermal stimulation are not equivalent to high-VDS OFF-state HCI, RF dynamic RON, or long-term gate TDDB. Qualification claims must remain mechanism-specific.

4. IGZO vertical charge-trap memory: useful Z-scaling evidence, but not a drop-in VNAND model

Source / reported
Yu-Jin Jung, Chi-Sun Hwang & Sung-Min Yoon, Enhancement in Charge-Trapping Efficiency for InGaZnO Vertical-Channel Memory Transistors via Gate-Stack Engineering: Validation in the 40 nm Scaling Regime, ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces 18, 46470–46482 (2026), DOI 10.1021/acsami.6c09875; online 21 Aug, issue 2 Sep 2026. The work uses amorphous IGZO deposited at 200°C and a nanocrystalline ZnO charge-trap layer in an ultra-scaled vertical CTM; efficient electrical erase is identified as a bottleneck.
Fig. 4 — gate-stack interpretation. Schematic / not to scale. This is deliberately separated from conventional poly-Si/SiN VNAND because channel and CTL materials change the transport regime.

VNAND translation. The valuable insight is not a direct retention lifetime number. It is that channel-material replacement can relieve one scaling constraint while moving the bottleneck into erase injection/barrier engineering. For poly-Si VNAND, the analogous question is whether a process split that improves channel conduction or Z-pitch silently changes tunnel/CTL charge injection and therefore retention/interference.

EXTRAPOLATION ONLY
IGZO channel + ZnO CTL → poly-Si channel + SiN CTL translation is qualitative only. Trap spectrum, band offsets, carrier polarity and erase transport are materially different.

5. SiC: a single online observable can be made mechanism-selective by controlled drive perturbation

Source / what is new
Haoran Zhang et al., A Dynamic Diagnosis Method for Separating Bond-Wires Fatigue and Gate Oxide Degradation of SiC MOSFETs, IET Power Electronics 19(1), e70286, first published 23 Jul 2026, DOI 10.1049/pel2.70286. A 1200 V/32 A TO-247-3 device is used. Both positive VTH shift and source-bond-wire parasitic increase reduce turn-on diD/dt, but sensitivity to oxide degradation depends mainly on VGH while bond-wire sensitivity depends mainly on RG.
Fig. 5 — diagnostic separability map. Source-reported sweep logic redrawn. HTGB at 150°C, VG=27 V shifted VTH from 3.01 to 4.08 V in the validation experiment; bond-wire fatigue was emulated by cutting source wires.
S = Δ(diD/dt)max,ratio;   ∂Soxide/∂VGH ≠ 0,   ∂Swire/∂RG ≠ 0

핵심은 precursor 하나가 두 failure mode에 민감하더라도, controlled perturbation에 대한 Jacobian이 다르면 mechanism을 분리할 수 있다는 것입니다. 이는 VNAND에서도 leakage, VTH, read-current 하나만 보지 말고 bias/read-condition perturbation을 통해 precursor의 mechanism selectivity를 높이는 설계로 확장할 수 있습니다.

Critical boundary
Bond-wire cutting과 HTGB는 equivalent degradation emulation입니다. 실제 power cycling에서 solder/clip/thermal-interface degradation이 함께 들어오면 2-mode separability가 유지되는지 재검증해야 합니다.

What changed our understanding?

  1. TDDB area conversion itself becomes a physics check. β와 면적만으로 자동 변환하기 전에 generation/annihilation competition이 없는지 확인해야 합니다.
  2. Acceleration experiments need state-reset logic. Sequential bias sweep는 독립 voltage points가 아니라 history-dependent trajectory일 수 있습니다.
  3. Mechanical/interface engineering can change electrical reliability without changing nominal bias. W37 CTN residual stress와 W39 GaN cap-strain 결과가 같은 방향의 cross-material evidence를 만듭니다.
  4. Precursor specificity can be engineered. observable 자체보다 controlled perturbation에 대한 sensitivity vector가 failure-mode 분리에 더 강력할 수 있습니다.

New testable hypotheses — Engineering Inference

H1. VNAND WL-to-WL/BEOL TDDB에서 area-normalized Weibull CDF가 stress time 또는 area에 따라 collapse하지 않는 split은 단순 extrinsic mixture뿐 아니라 defect annihilation/recovery state를 포함할 수 있다. Falsification: area·time across splits가 single β/η scaling으로 완전히 collapse하고 precursor recovery가 없으면 H1은 기각.

H2. VNAND GIDL/erase 또는 HV stress의 stepped-bias qualification에서 이전 stress가 만든 trap occupancy가 다음 step의 apparent acceleration factor를 왜곡할 수 있다. Falsification: fresh-population parallel stress와 reversed-order stress가 동일 kinetics/η/β를 주면 history effect는 무시 가능.

H3. 한 개의 read-current/leakage precursor도 두 개 이상의 read-bias 또는 pulse-edge perturbation을 주면 trap/reversible-charge와 permanent damage를 분리할 수 있다. Falsification: perturbation Jacobian이 두 failure modes에서 collinear하면 분리 불가.
