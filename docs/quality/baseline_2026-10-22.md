## What the table does and does not say

Facts measured:

- No W37 to W43 report contains the patterns of `leak-check`. All DOIs are well formed and resolve at Crossref; the W40 ledger titles match the Crossref titles.
- W40 pairs every Korean paragraph with an English one (153 of 153).

Gaps (the real content of this baseline):

1. Traceability gap. Only W40 has a ledger, and the ledger holds 20 SOURCE_REPORTED abstract statements. W37 to W39 and W41 to W43 have 0 claims, so no number in them can be traced to a source location by a program.
2. Vacuous vocabulary checks. W37 to W39 and W41 to W43 use none of the five labels and no evidence grades, so the vocabulary PASS says nothing about their rigor. This is a content gap, not a pass.
3. Bilingual pairing is unmeasured for W37 to W39 and W41 to W43, because they do not mark languages with `lang` attributes. Whether they are bilingual in some other layout was not checked by this linter.
4. Numbers outside the ledger in W40. 26 numeric tokens are not in any claim: 14 of 40 distinct tokens match. I inspected 5 of the 26 (0.21, 26, 37, 59, 70): 26, 37 and 59 are volume or issue numbers in the citation lines, and 0.21 and 70 are values computed inside the report. The other 21 were not inspected one by one; by pattern the list also contains page numbers, an HTTP status and article numbers, but that is an expectation, not a verification. The WARN is therefore a worklist, not an error count.
5. Re-runnable derivations: 0 of 5 W40 audit scripts declare `RERUN_OK`, and sympy is not installed on the host that produced this baseline, so no script was re-run and no derivation output was compared. The five stored outputs are unverified by this linter.
6. Detection power is only demonstrated by synthetic unit tests (placeholder-like and leaking inputs fail; clean inputs pass). It was not measured against real past leaks. A zero in `leak-check` means "none of the five fixed patterns".
7. The ledger itself rests on one grade convention (grade C for abstract restatements, taken from the W40 draft) that needs the owner's confirmation.

Suggested order of work (suggestion, not a measurement): label and grade usage in W41 onward, then ledger coverage for new weeks, then re-runnable derivation headers.
