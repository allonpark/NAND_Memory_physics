English. VNAND analysis uses cylindrical radial geometry and a poly-Si channel. The field obeys E(r)∝1/(εr), with ΔV=∫E(r)dr; planar oxide expressions are not substituted without validation. Stress-to-use transfer is withheld when material, polarity, geometry, failure criterion or trap regime changes.

3. Model-based read-reference-voltage compensation for cross-temperature reliability in 3D TLC NAND flash memory

2026-09-25 · IEICE Electronics Express / full PDF · Primary source

Source / What is new · Physical causal chain

저자들은 on-chip 온도 보정을 끄고 85°C/10h bake 후 −40~120°C(20°C step) read를 수행했다. Tco를 P/E cycle, state-center, layer에 따라 보정했으며 extreme case RBER 24.28%→0.44%를 측정했다. SSDsim 평균 응답은 774.770→435.362 μs로 43.81% 감소했다.

English. The authors disabled on-chip temperature compensation, baked at 85°C for 10 h, and read from −40 to 120°C in 20°C steps. Tco accounts for P/E cycles, state center and layer. Extreme-case RBER improved from 24.28% to 0.44%; SSDsim mean response fell from 774.770 to 435.362 μs (43.81%).

Equation & scaling

Δμ≈−Tco(C,Vs,L)(Tread−Tprogram); Tco=Tco(L)+ΔTco(C)+ΔTco(Vs) [source model; Tco V/K]

Structure translation & competing signatures

온도가 오르면 poly-Si channel current가 증가하여 fixed-current read criterion의 apparent VTH가 낮아질 수 있다. 이는 실제 trapped electron loss와 다르다. Program T 변화는 verify feedback을 통해 실제 stored charge도 변화시킬 수 있다. SSDsim latency는 silicon 측정이 아니다.

English. Higher temperature can increase poly-Si channel current and reduce apparent VTH at fixed sensing current; this differs from trapped-electron loss. Program-temperature changes can also alter stored charge through verify feedback. SSDsim latency is not a direct silicon measurement.

DOE, failure mode, falsification

DOE: Tprogram × Tread × P/E × state × layer × retention delay; VTH CDF, RBER, read-retry. 반증: 동일 ΔT에서 absolute T/delay에 따라 residual이 커진다.

English. DOE: program/read temperature × P/E × state × layer × retention delay; VTH CDF, RBER and read retries. Falsification: large residual dependence on absolute temperature or delay at fixed ΔT.

English. The study non-destructively infers trap-depth distributions from frequency-temperature gate-admittance response during negative-CVS TDDB in SiC MOS structures. Higher stress changes not only trap density but also the spatial extent and centroid of active oxide defects.

English. Bulk oxide traps, interface states, ionic dispersion and measurement artifacts can overlap in admittance dispersion. VFB, Dit, recovery and trap-depth centroid complement Ig precursors in locating damage.

What Changed Our Understanding

① Margin budget에 interaction term을 실험 변수로 추가한다. ② PINN inverse model에는 parameter identifiability gate가 필요하다. ③ Read-temperature-induced apparent VTH shift를 irreversible charge loss와 분리한다. ④ Cryogenic detrapping과 hot retention을 동일 Ea로 묶지 않는다. ⑤ TDDB에는 trap population뿐 아니라 depth centroid도 precursor 후보가 된다.

English. (1) Test margin interaction rather than assuming additivity. (2) Require an identifiability gate for PINN inversion. (3) Separate read-temperature apparent VTH shifts from irreversible charge loss. (4) Do not merge cryogenic detrapping and hot retention under one Ea. (5) Treat trap-depth centroid as a TDDB precursor candidate alongside trap population.

New Testable Hypotheses & Falsification

H1 · Engineering Inference: 동일 PGM VTH라도 GB 구조가 다르면 T/rise-time sensitivity가 다르다. 반증: 독립 observable과 posterior가 동일하다.

English. Identical PGM VTH with different grain-boundary structures yields different temperature/rise-time sensitivities. Falsification: independent observables and parameter posteriors coincide.

H2 · Engineering Inference: Same final VTH에서 read-T reversible component와 bake-persistent component는 다른 failure population이다. 반증: recovery/time/T signatures가 일치한다.

English. Reversible read-temperature shifts and bake-persistent shifts at matched final VTH reflect different populations. Falsification: matching recovery/time/temperature signatures.

H3 · Engineering Inference: Trap centroid가 total Ntrap보다 held-out tBD prediction에 추가 정보를 준다. 반증: out-of-sample 성능 향상이 없다.

English. Trap centroid adds held-out tBD predictive information beyond total Ntrap. Falsification: no out-of-sample improvement.

Design / Qualification Implications

VNAND: poly-Si GB, SiN CTL connectivity, tunnel/blocking oxide radial field, WL metal RC, SSL/GSL bias, stack height를 함께 기록한다. HV: Ig, Qinj, VFB, Dit, recovery, failure location, β/η를 함께 본다. Screen-to-use는 mechanism continuity 확인 전 승인하지 않는다.

English. VNAND: jointly record poly-Si GB, SiN CTL connectivity, tunnel/blocking oxide radial field, WL-metal RC, SSL/GSL bias and stack height. HV: monitor Ig, injected charge, VFB, Dit, recovery, failure location and β/η. Do not authorize screen-to-use conversion before mechanism continuity.
